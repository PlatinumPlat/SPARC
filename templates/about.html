About

OUR MISSION
SPARC is a student-led initiative that aims to advocate for, facilitate, and promote early detection of scoliosis in an accessible manner.

I'm Sophia, a Grade 9 student in the Pre-IB program at St. Robert CHS in Thornhill, Ontario.

After my identical twin sister Olivia was diagnosed with severe AIS (Adolescent Idiopathic Scoliosis) that required surgery, I wanted to help others avoid the risk of needing surgery by promoting early detection.

In particular, discovering scoliosis at a mild severity can allow for non-surgical interventions like bracing and physical therapy.

To learn more about me and the projects I have built, please visit
my personal website at https://platinumplat.github.io/Sophia-s-Space/
and my Github at https://github.com/PlatinumPlat.
